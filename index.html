Lovable Birds

Mountain Blue Bird

The mountain bluebird is a small migratory thrush found in western North America.

Black-Capped Chickadee

A small North American songbird that commonly lives in deciduous and mixed forests.

Pardalote

Small and brightly coloured birds native to Australia, known for their short tails and strong legs.

Water Birds

Albatross

Albatrosses are large seagoing birds known for their enormous wingspans and long distance flights.

Kingfisher

Brightly coloured birds famous for diving into water to catch fish.

Penguin

Flightless aquatic birds specially adapted for swimming and surviving in cold environments.

LIFE CYCLE

Life Cycle Of Birds

1. The Egg
2. The Hatching
3. The Nesting
4. The Fledgeling
5. The Juvenile
6. The Sub Adult
7. The Adult
1

The Egg

All birds start their lives inside an egg. The developing embryo grows inside the shell while the parent birds protect and incubate it.

2

The Hatching

After breaking through the eggshell, the newly hatched bird is called a hatchling. It depends heavily on its parents for protection.

3

The Nesting

During this stage, the young bird develops feathers, strength and the early skills required for flight.

4

The Fledgeling

The fledgling develops stronger wings and begins leaving the nest while still receiving support from its parents.

5

The Juvenile

The young bird continues to grow and gradually develops the characteristics of an adult bird.

6

The Sub Adult

The bird becomes increasingly independent while developing adult plumage and physical characteristics.

7

The Adult

The adult bird reaches maturity, develops its final plumage and becomes capable of reproduction.
